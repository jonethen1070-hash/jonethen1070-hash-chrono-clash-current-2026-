Gameplay SFX are original Chrono Clash 48 kHz stereo crystal hits
(not 22 kHz sine stand-ins). Player gem swaps and clears use layered
glass impact, metallic resonance, and round-robin variants. The rival
board has no local match/break SFX.

Gameplay voice callouts (Locked / Combo / Ultimate) are short neural
human VO, mixed above crystal SFX on a dedicated bus.
