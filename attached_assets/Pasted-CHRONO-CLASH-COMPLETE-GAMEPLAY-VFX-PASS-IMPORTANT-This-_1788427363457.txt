==================================================
MAIN GOAL
==================================================

Make Chrono Clash feel like a premium futuristic competitive puzzle game.

The player should receive an immediate visual response for every important action.

VFX should follow this feeling:

TOUCH
→ GEM RESPONSE
→ MATCH
→ IMPACT
→ CASCADE
→ COMBO
→ ENERGY
→ ABILITY
→ BATTLE RESPONSE

Every effect must be:
- fast
- crisp
- smooth
- readable
- satisfying
- mobile-friendly

Do NOT make the screen constantly flashy.

Effects happen mainly when something important occurs.

==================================================
1. GEM TOUCH / SELECTION
==================================================

When the player touches/selects a gem:

- subtle bright outline
- small scale pulse
- soft glow
- tiny particle sparkle
- very short response time

The response must feel immediate.

Do NOT permanently enlarge gems.

Do NOT change gem size/layout.

==================================================
2. GEM MATCH
==================================================

When a valid match occurs:

First:
- matched gems briefly brighten
- quick energy flash
- subtle pulse

Then:
- gems disappear with a crisp burst
- small particles radiate outward
- tiny fragments/sparkles
- short glow flash

The effect should feel like the gem is breaking apart from energy.

Use color-aware particles based on the gem type.

Keep the effect centered on the matched gems.

Do NOT cover the entire board.

==================================================
3. MATCH IMPACT
==================================================

Create a very short impact flash when a match is confirmed.

Use:
- bright center flash
- radial particles
- subtle ring/shockwave
- tiny screen/board impact

The impact must be extremely short.

Avoid excessive screen shake.

The player should feel:

"YES — I made that match."

==================================================
4. CASCADE / FALLING GEMS
==================================================

When gems fall after a match:

- smooth falling animation
- subtle motion trail
- tiny glow during movement
- soft landing pulse

When new gems enter the board:

- quick materialize effect
- small sparkle
- subtle upward energy shimmer

Do NOT slow gameplay excessively.

Animations must remain responsive.

==================================================
5. COMBO SYSTEM VISUALS
==================================================

If the game already has combo/multiple-match logic:

Increase VFX intensity gradually.

Example:

MATCH 1
small impact

MATCH 2
stronger particles + glow

MATCH 3
stronger burst + brighter energy

HIGH COMBO
larger but still controlled impact

Do NOT invent or change scoring rules.

Only react visually to the existing combo state.

If there is no combo state available, do NOT modify gameplay logic just to create one.

==================================================
6. ENERGY GAIN
==================================================

When the player gains energy:

- small energy particles travel toward the ENERGY meter
- meter receives a short glow pulse
- number briefly highlights
- tiny blue/cyan spark effect

The visual should communicate:

"That match gave me energy."

Do NOT change energy values or calculations.

==================================================
7. ENERGY BURST
==================================================

When Energy Burst is successfully used:

Create a clearly recognizable ability effect.

Sequence:

1. Ability card flashes.
2. Energy travels from the HUD toward the board.
3. 3x3 affected area receives a cyan/green energy grid highlight.
4. Short charging pulse.
5. Strong radial explosion.
6. Affected gems break with particles.
7. Energy shockwave expands outward.
8. Board settles with a short residual glow.

Make this feel substantially stronger than a normal match.

Do NOT change the existing 3x3 gameplay logic.

The VFX must follow the actual affected cells.

==================================================
8. MEGA STRIKE
==================================================

When Mega Strike is successfully used:

Make it the most visually powerful normal ability.

Sequence:

1. Ability card flashes.
2. Red/magenta energy charges.
3. Affected color is highlighted across the board.
4. Short warning pulse.
5. Energy beam/flash sweeps through the affected gems.
6. Gems explode simultaneously.
7. Particles travel outward.
8. Short red/magenta shockwave.
9. Board settles.

It should feel like a powerful weapon.

Do NOT change which gems are affected.

Use the existing ability logic.

==================================================
9. FREEZE
==================================================

When FREEZE is activated:

- cyan/ice energy spreads over the board
- subtle frost particles
- affected board edges glow
- brief crystalline/frozen shimmer
- activation button pulses once

Keep the effect elegant.

Do NOT cover gem readability.

Do NOT change Freeze mechanics.

==================================================
10. TIME SHIFT
==================================================

When TIME SHIFT is activated:

Use a distinct time-manipulation effect:

- purple circular energy ring
- short reverse-time particles
- subtle radial distortion
- brief clock/time pulse
- particles move backward briefly
- board receives a short purple glow

Do NOT alter the actual gameplay logic.

The effect should communicate:

TIME IS BEING MANIPULATED.

==================================================
11. REWIND
==================================================

When REWIND is activated:

Create a stronger temporal effect:

- red/purple circular rewind ring
- particles briefly move backward
- short rotational energy trail
- board receives a temporal pulse
- quick flash as the previous state returns

Do NOT modify the underlying rewind functionality.

Only visualize it.

==================================================
12. CPU / RIVAL ACTION
==================================================

When the CPU makes an important action:

Give the player visual feedback.

Examples:

- subtle red pulse around Rival HUD
- red energy travelling toward the board
- brief board-side red flash
- affected gems receive a short warning glow
- impact when the CPU action resolves

Do NOT make the CPU unfairly obvious.

Do NOT expose hidden CPU information.

Do NOT change CPU behavior.

==================================================
13. PLAYER VS RIVAL FEEL
==================================================

The game should visually communicate:

PLAYER = CYAN / BLUE ENERGY

RIVAL = RED / MAGENTA ENERGY

Player actions should primarily use cyan/blue.

Rival actions should primarily use red/magenta.

Temporal abilities can use purple.

This creates a consistent visual language.

==================================================
14. BOARD AMBIENT VFX
==================================================

Add very subtle ambient movement around the board:

- tiny floating particles
- occasional energy spark
- subtle cyan edge energy
- subtle red edge energy
- extremely slow atmospheric movement

This must NOT become distracting.

The board should feel alive even when nothing is happening.

Do NOT animate every gem continuously.

==================================================
15. BUTTON FEEDBACK
==================================================

Existing game controls should feel responsive.

When tapped:

- tiny press animation
- short glow
- icon pulse
- quick release animation

Do NOT resize or reposition buttons.

Do NOT change their locked dimensions.

==================================================
16. VICTORY
==================================================

When the player wins:

Create a strong reward sequence.

Suggested sequence:

- board energy pulse
- cyan particle burst
- expanding energy ring
- bright but brief screen glow
- particles rise outward/upward
- victory UI receives a premium glow

The result should feel earned.

Do NOT change victory logic.

==================================================
17. DEFEAT
==================================================

When the player loses:

Use a different emotional treatment:

- red/magenta energy pulse
- brief board dim
- particles fall/dissipate
- controlled red glow
- subtle temporal fade

Do not make defeat visually frustrating or overly dark.

==================================================
18. TRANSITIONS
==================================================

Use smooth transitions between important game states.

Avoid instant hard cuts where an existing transition is expected.

Keep transitions short.

Target approximately:

100–300ms for small UI feedback.

300–700ms for major ability/result effects.

Do NOT delay gameplay unnecessarily.
