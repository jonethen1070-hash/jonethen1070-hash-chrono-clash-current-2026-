CHRONO CLASH — POWER ABILITY VFX MASTER PASS
DESIGNER-LEVEL IMPLEMENTATION

I want you to treat this as if you are the VFX director and gameplay designer responsible for the final visual identity of Chrono Clash.

We are polishing ONLY these three existing powers:

1. ENERGY BURST
2. MEGA STRIKE / MEGA BLAST
3. REWIND

The goal is premium, spectacular, readable, fast and satisfying — AAA-inspired mobile game quality with Chrono Clash's crystalline sci-fi identity.

IMPORTANT:
The powers must actually WORK through the existing gameplay system.
Do not create fake visual buttons or cosmetic-only effects.

==================================================
CORE DESIGN RULE
==================================================

Every power must have a clear visual story:

PLAYER ACTIVATES
→ POWER CHARGES
→ TARGET / BOARD RESPONSE
→ POWER IMPACT
→ GEM DESTRUCTION / BOARD EFFECT
→ ENERGY DISSIPATES
→ BOARD RETURNS TO NORMAL

The player must immediately understand:
"MY POWER ACTIVATED"
"THIS IS WHAT IT DID"
"THAT WAS POWERFUL"

Keep the entire sequence fast and responsive.

NO LONG INPUT LOCKS.
NO PLAYER-FACING LOCKED STATE.
NO FREEZING THE GAME.

If internal resolution sequencing is necessary, keep player input responsive and queue safely rather than blocking the player.

==================================================
1. ENERGY BURST
==================================================

DESIGN INTENT:

Energy Burst should feel like a concentrated release of stored crystalline energy.

It should be:
FAST
BRIGHT
POWERFUL
CLEAN
SATISFYING

NOT:
a generic explosion
a fireball
a screen flash
a noisy particle cloud

ACTIVATION:

When the player activates Energy Burst:

0–80ms:
- selected power button reacts immediately
- compact energy charge forms
- crystalline particles pull inward
- small radial glow builds around the power origin

80–160ms:
- energy reaches peak charge
- subtle cyan/white core
- thin energy rings form
- tiny crystalline fragments orbit briefly

IMPACT:

At release:
- sharp radial energy burst
- expanding luminous ring
- short crystalline shockwave
- affected gems receive a visible energy hit

The impact should be localized and readable.

GEMS:

Affected gems should:
- brighten rapidly
- develop internal energy cracks
- fracture
- break into small crystal fragments
- dissolve/fade cleanly

Do NOT make every gem explode independently with huge particles.

Use a controlled synchronized destruction wave.

Timing target:
approximately 150–250ms total visual impact.

AFTER EFFECT:

After the burst:
- particles continue outward briefly
- energy fragments fade
- glow collapses naturally
- board becomes visually clean again

No permanent glow.

==================================================
2. MEGA STRIKE / MEGA BLAST
==================================================

This is the HERO power.

It must feel substantially more powerful than Energy Burst.

DESIGN INTENT:

Mega Strike should feel like a massive futuristic crystalline weapon activating inside the arena.

Think:
"the arena itself just fired a devastating energy strike."

ACTIVATION:

0–100ms:
- power button reacts immediately
- arena lighting subtly responds
- energy begins gathering toward the strike origin
- surrounding gems receive a subtle light response

100–250ms:
- large concentrated energy core forms
- multiple thin crystalline energy rings
- directional energy lines converge
- bright core builds

Do NOT hold the player for a long cinematic.

Maximum anticipation:
~250ms.

STRIKE:

Release with a powerful directional impact.

Use:
- bright crystalline core
- sharp energy beam/arc
- expanding shockwave
- controlled particle burst
- crystal shards
- short-lived light streaks

The strike should have a strong beginning and extremely fast impact.

IMPACT TIMING:
~80–140ms.

BOARD RESPONSE:

Affected gems should respond in a cascading visual sequence.

Example:

STRIKE
↓
impact wave
↓
gems flash
↓
cracks appear
↓
gems fracture
↓
crystal shards
↓
clear
↓
gravity/cascade

Do not simply delete the gems.

The player must visually understand the destructive power.

==================================================
MEGA STRIKE — SCREEN FEEDBACK
==================================================

Use VERY subtle global feedback:

- brief arena brightness lift
- tiny camera/board impulse if existing architecture supports it
- very subtle screen shake

Maximum screen shake:
~2–4px equivalent.

Duration:
~60–90ms.

Do NOT make the entire screen shake violently.

The effect should feel expensive, not chaotic.

==================================================
3. REWIND
==================================================

Rewind should feel completely different from destructive powers.

DESIGN INTENT:

The player is bending time.

Visual language:
TEMPORAL
CRYSTALLINE
FUTURISTIC
CONTROLLED

Use:
- cyan/blue temporal trails
- ghost images
- reverse-moving particles
- subtle circular time rings
- crystalline distortion
- backward energy motion

SEQUENCE:

ACTIVATION
→ temporal ring
→ board state visually pulls backward
→ gems travel along reverse trajectories
→ previous state restored
→ temporal energy collapses

The effect must communicate:

"TIME IS MOVING BACKWARD."

Do NOT simply teleport the board to its previous state.

==================================================
4. POWER COLOR LANGUAGE
==================================================

Use consistent Chrono Clash visual language.

Energy Burst:
CYAN / WHITE

Mega Strike:
CYAN + WHITE CORE + controlled deeper energy accents

Rewind:
CYAN / BLUE temporal energy

Highlights should approach white at peak intensity.

Avoid flat neon colors.

The gems themselves must remain recognizable.

==================================================
5. GEM DESTRUCTION QUALITY
==================================================

Power destruction should reuse the premium crystalline destruction system.

Use:
- internal crack
- bright core
- small shards
- tiny fragments
- controlled energy particles
- smooth fade

No cheap generic particle explosion.

No giant opaque smoke.

No cartoon fire.

The gem should look like an illuminated crystal being broken by energy.

==================================================
6. CASCADE SYNERGY
==================================================

Power effects must transition naturally into the existing match/cascade system.

Example:

POWER IMPACT
↓
GEMS BREAK
↓
BOARD SETTLES
↓
MATCH DETECTED
↓
MATCH VFX
↓
CASCADE
↓
BOARD RESTABILIZES

Do NOT create duplicate match VFX.

Do NOT trigger duplicate Match SFX.

Do NOT interfere with the existing match-wave audio progression.

==================================================
7. AUDIO MUST REMAIN SEPARATE
==================================================

Do not replace or redesign audio.

Keep:
- Arena Pulse
- approved Swap SFX
- approved Match SFX
- existing power audio

VFX timing should synchronize naturally with the existing audio events.

Do not add random sounds.

==================================================
8. VFX MOTION QUALITY
==================================================

All VFX must use smooth time-based animation.

Prefer:
- cubic ease-out
- cubic ease-in-out
- exponential decay
- controlled spring only where appropriate

Avoid:
- linear movement
- instant opacity changes
- frame-count timing
- excessive bouncing

Every effect needs:

START
→ BUILD
→ PEAK
→ DECAY

==================================================
9. PARTICLE DISCIPLINE
==================================================

Premium does NOT mean maximum particles.

Use fewer, better particles.

Particles should have:
- short lifetime
- directional motion
- size variation
- alpha fade
- color variation within the power palette

Avoid particle spam.

Mobile performance is critical.

Use pooling/reuse where practical.

No large per-frame allocations.

==================================================
10. BOARD READABILITY
==================================================

During every power:

The player must still understand:
- which gems are affected
- what the power did
- where the board is
- what happens next

Never cover the entire board with opaque VFX.

Keep the center of the action bright while preserving gem silhouettes.

==================================================
11. BUTTON FEEDBACK
==================================================

When a power is activated:

The corresponding power button should have a polished response:

PRESS
→ glow
→ energy transfer
→ activation

Use:
- subtle scale response
- controlled glow
- energy pulse moving away from the button

Do not redesign the button.

Do not add UI clutter.

Player tap

WAIT
FROZEN
