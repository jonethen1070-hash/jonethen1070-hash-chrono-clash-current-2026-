VFX MASTER PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Every power interaction must feel like ENERGY interacting with REAL CRYSTALLINE MATERIAL.

Think:

ENERGY SOURCE
→ ENERGY TRAVEL
→ ENERGY CONCENTRATION
→ LIGHT RESPONSE
→ CRYSTAL RESPONSE
→ FRACTURE
→ IMPACT
→ SHARDS
→ DISSIPATION

Everything should have cause and effect.

If energy moves toward a gem,

If it breaks,
shards should inherit the direction and force of the impact.

This is what makes VFX feel physical rather than decorative.
