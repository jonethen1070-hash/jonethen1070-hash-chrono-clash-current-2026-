Upgrade ONLY the gem VFX to make the gems feel premium, colorful, crystalline, and cinematic.

Do NOT change gameplay, gem positions, matching logic, Mega Strike logic, scoring, or board behavior.

GEM APPEARANCE

Make every gem feel like a dimensional crystal:

* Strong 3D/crystalline depth
* Transparent or semi-transparent crystal edges
* Internal colored energy/glow
* Bright specular highlights
* Subtle refraction/shine
* Clean silhouette so the gem remains easy to recognize
* Each gem color should have its own luminous energy identity

WHEN A GEM IS CLEARED

When a gem disappears:

1. The gem briefly becomes brighter.
2. Add a fast internal energy pulse.
3. Create a small colorful flash matching that gem’s color.
4. The crystal visually fractures/cracks for a split second.
5. The gem bursts into small glowing crystal shards.
6. Add matching-color particles flying outward.
7. Add a thin expanding energy ring.
8. Add a few tiny sparks/embers.
9. Fade everything smoothly and quickly.

The entire effect should feel like the crystal is exploding with energy, not simply being deleted.

COLOR-MATCHED VFX

Blue gem → cyan/blue crystal shards and energy
Red gem → red/crimson energy
Green gem → emerald/neon-green energy
Yellow gem → golden/electric-yellow energy
Purple gem → violet/cosmic-purple energy

The VFX color must come from the actual gem’s color/type dynamically.

MULTIPLE GEMS

When multiple gems disappear:

* Give each gem its own localized explosion.
* Synchronize the timing slightly so it feels cinematic.
* Add a subtle wave of energy passing through the clearing gems.
* Do not merge all effects into one giant effect that hides the board.

MEGA STRIKE

When Mega Strike clears all gems matching the selected gem’s color:

* Each matching gem gets the same premium destruction VFX.
* Add a visible energy pulse traveling between the matching gems.
* The selected color should dominate the entire effect.
* Non-selected-color gems must receive no destruction VFX and must remain visible.

QUALITY

Aim for:
premium AAA-inspired crystalline sci-fi VFX — bright, colorful, sharp, energetic, cinematic, but clean and readable.

Keep effects short and responsive so the game remains smooth on mobile.

First inspect the existing gem rendering and VFX system and enhance it rather than replacing working gameplay code.
