PREMIUM SWIPE FEEL:

1. DIRECT FINGER RESPONSE
- The selected gem must follow the swipe intention immediately.
- Do not make the player wait for a threshold after the swipe direction is clearly established.
- Preserve the existing valid-swipe threshold so accidental taps do not trigger swaps.

2. PHYSICAL SWAP
- Use the existing renderer/animation system.
- The two gems should travel toward each other's cells with smooth acceleration/deceleration.
- Target roughly 110–140ms for the committed swap.
- Avoid linear movement.
- Avoid slow floating movement.

3. PREMIUM "SNAP"
- At the end of the swap, add a tiny positional settle/snap.
- Extremely subtle: approximately 2–4px visual movement at most.
- No exaggerated bounce.
- The gem should feel like it has weight and has landed securely into its socket.

4. MICRO-SCALE RESPONSE
- During the final few milliseconds of the swap, allow a very subtle scale response, approximately 1–2%.
- Return immediately to normal scale.
- Do NOT make the gems look like they are pulsing.

5. MATCH TRANSITION
When the swipe creates a match:
- The completed swap should visually finish first.
- Then transition cleanly into the existing match/cascade presentation.
- Preserve the existing 64ms impact lock and crystalline destruction treatment.
- Do not redesign the cascade.

6. INVALID SWIPE
- Invalid swaps should feel equally polished.
- Smoothly return the gems to their original cells.
- Target roughly 100–130ms return.
- Add a very small settle at the end.
- Never leave a gem visually between cells.

7. RAPID INPUT
- Test fast repeated swipes.
- Do not allow animation state corruption.
- Existing input locks/rules must remain authoritative.
