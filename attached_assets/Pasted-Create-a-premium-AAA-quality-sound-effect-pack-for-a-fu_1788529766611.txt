Create a premium AAA-quality sound-effect pack for a futuristic crystalline sci-fi match-3 mobile game called "Chrono Clash".

SOUND DESIGN STYLE:
Premium, sharp, tactile, futuristic, crystalline, energetic and satisfying.
Think polished AAA mobile game sound design — NOT cartoonish, childish, generic arcade beeps, or cheap electronic sounds.

CRITICAL:
Create CLEAN, SHORT, INDIVIDUAL GAME SOUND EFFECTS with clear separation between each sound.
No music.
No melody.
No vocals.
No voice.
No ambience.
No background noise.

SFX REQUIRED:

1. GEM SWAP
A very short tactile crystal snap + subtle polished metallic click.
Bright, precise and satisfying.
Approximately 80–120ms.

2. GEM MATCH
A single bright crystalline strike/chime with a tiny sparkling tail.
It should feel rewarding and immediately communicate "MATCH".
Approximately 180–250ms.
ONE clear hit only — not multiple repeated hits.

3. GEM BREAK
Sharp crystalline fracture followed by tiny crystal shards/sparkles.
Energetic but controlled.
Approximately 200–350ms.
No huge explosion and no cartoon glass-breaking sound.

4. CASCADE
A variation of the match sound designed for consecutive match waves.
Keep the same Chrono Clash crystal identity, but progressively raise the pitch slightly for successive cascades.
Each cascade should still be ONE clean sound.

5. BIG COMBO
A stronger crystalline impact with a deeper futuristic layer underneath and bright crystal sparkle on top.
Powerful but premium.
Reserved for significant combos.

6. ENERGY BURST
Futuristic energy charge followed by a powerful crystalline release.
Short, energetic and impactful.

7. MEGA STRIKE
Heavy futuristic impact combined with a sharp crystalline energy strike.
Powerful and dramatic without becoming noisy.

8. REWIND
Distinctive futuristic time-reversal sound:
short reverse crystalline sweep + subtle temporal shimmer.
Clean and recognizable.

9. VICTORY
Very short premium ascending crystalline fanfare.
Triumphant and futuristic.
No long musical composition.

IMPORTANT AUDIO DESIGN RULES:
- Every SFX must be short and clean.
- Strong transient followed by controlled decay.
- Preserve clarity on phone speakers.
- Avoid excessive bass.
- Avoid harsh high-frequency noise.
- Avoid huge reverberation.
- Avoid long tails that overlap other game sounds.
- Sounds must remain recognizable when played at low volume.
- Crystal sounds should feel physical and dimensional.
- Use subtle futuristic electronic layers only where appropriate.
- The overall sound palette must feel like ONE cohesive game.

MOST IMPORTANT FOR GEM GAMEPLAY:
Touching a gem = NO SOUND.
Selecting a gem = NO SOUND.
Dragging a gem = NO SOUND.
Invalid swap = NO SOUND.

Only:
VALID SWAP → ONE swap sound.
MATCH WAVE → ONE match sound.
BREAK → break sound when appropriate.
CASCADE → ONE sound per separate cascade wave.

Do NOT create three match sounds because three gems matched.
A 3-gem match must produce ONE match sound for the entire match event.

Deliver clean isolated SFX suitable for implementation in a real mobile game.
