==================================================
1. MATCH CONFIRMATION
==================================================

When a match is detected:

Give the matched gems a very short visual confirmation.

Approximately 60–90ms.

Each matched crystal should:
- slightly tighten/compress
- brighten from inside
- emphasize its facets
- produce a subtle energy pulse

Keep this extremely responsive.

Do NOT freeze the player.

==================================================
2. CRYSTAL FRACTURE
==================================================

Before the gem disappears, make it visually fracture.

The crystal should briefly look like its internal energy is cracking apart.

Use the existing Canvas renderer/VFX infrastructure.

Possible techniques:
- bright facet lines
- radial fracture strokes
- internal crack flashes
- tiny crystalline highlights

The effect should be visible on a real phone screen.

Do NOT use a generic opacity fade.

==================================================
3. CRYSTAL BREAK
==================================================

Create a short, sharp destruction moment.

Target approximately 100–150ms.

Sequence:

CHARGE
→ FRACTURE
→ BREAK

At the break:
- crystal contracts slightly
- bright crystalline core flashes
- small shards eject outward
- tiny particles follow
- short localized energy burst occurs

The gem then disappears.

The effect should remain localized to the matched gem.

==================================================
4. SHARDS MUST LOOK LIKE CRYSTAL
==================================================

Do not make generic circular particles the main effect.

Use a mixture of:
- tiny angular shards
- short crystalline fragments
- small spark particles

Fragments should inherit the gem's color with a brighter/whiter core.

Example:

cyan gem
→ cyan + white crystal fragments

magenta gem
→ magenta + white fragments

yellow gem
→ gold + white fragments

green gem
→ green + white fragments

blue gem
→ blue + white fragments

purple gem
→ purple + white fragments

==================================================
5. IMPACT RING
==================================================

Add a very short localized impact ring at the socket.

It should:
- appear at the moment of break
- expand quickly
- fade smoothly

Keep it thin and crisp.

Do NOT create a giant glowing circle.

==================================================
6. PARTICLE MOTION
==================================================

Particles should have intentional motion.

Avoid random noise.

Use:
- short outward velocity
- slight directional bias
- quick deceleration
- very short lifetime

The player should perceive an actual impact.

Do not flood the board with particles.

==================================================
7. MATCH SIZE RESPONSE
==================================================

Make larger matches feel slightly more powerful.

3 gems:
- standard effect

4 gems:
- slightly stronger impact
- slightly more shards

5+ gems:
- stronger energy pulse
- slightly larger localized burst

Do NOT create huge explosions.

==================================================
8. MULTI-GEM TIMING
==================================================

For a group of matched gems, do not make every crystal explode on exactly the same frame.

Use a subtle 10–25ms stagger between nearby gems.

This should create a satisfying chain reaction:

CRACK → CRACK → CRACK

rather than:

BOOM ALL AT ONCE

Keep the entire match extremely fast.

==================================================
9. CASCADE ESCALATION
==================================================

For automatic cascade matches:

Wave 1:
normal impact

Wave 2:
slightly stronger

Wave 3:
slightly stronger again

Increase intensity through:
- slightly more particles
- slightly brighter core
- slightly stronger impact ring

Do NOT increase duration significantly.

==================================================
10. AFTERGLOW
==================================================

After the crystal breaks:

Leave a very brief residual energy trace at the socket.

Approximately 40–70ms.

Then clear it completely.

The socket should feel like it received an energy impact.
