CORE GEM DESTRUCTION

When a gem is cleared, make it feel like a powerful crystalline object is being destroyed by contained energy:

1. Anticipation
    * Gem briefly intensifies in brightness.
    * Internal energy rapidly builds inside the crystal.
    * Add a subtle scale pulse.
    * Add a sharp specular highlight sweeping across the crystal.
2. Crystal fracture
    * At the exact destruction moment, create a very brief crystalline crack/fracture impression.
    * The crystal should appear to break from the inside rather than simply vanish.
3. Energy detonation
    * Bright central flash.
    * Multiple layers of energy expanding outward.
    * Thin luminous shockwave ring.
    * Small secondary energy rings.
    * Strong but extremely short bloom.
4. Crystal shards
    * Generate visually convincing small crystal fragments.
    * Fragments should inherit the gem’s actual color.
    * Shards should shoot outward with varied velocity and rotation.
    * Add subtle motion trails to the fastest shards.
    * Shards should catch light as they rotate.
    * Fade naturally rather than simply disappearing.
5. Energy particles
    * Use multiple particle scales:
        * large glowing fragments
        * medium crystal particles
        * tiny sparks
        * fine energy dust
    * Give particles varied trajectories and lifetimes.
    * Avoid a uniform circular particle explosion.
6. Afterglow
    * Leave a tiny residual energy glow for a fraction of a second.
    * Then smoothly dissipate.
