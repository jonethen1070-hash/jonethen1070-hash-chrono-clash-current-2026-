Improve the COLOR CONTRAST and COLOR SEPARATION of the gems.

Use the current gem designs as the base. Do NOT replace the gem artwork or change gameplay.

The goal is for every gem color to be instantly recognizable on the board, even during fast movement, cascades, and VFX.

CURRENT PROBLEM

The gems are very bright and heavily glowing, so some colors visually blend together.

I want:

* stronger color identity
* clearer separation between gem types
* better readability at a glance
* premium crystalline materials
* controlled glow instead of color washout

COLOR IDENTITY

Give each gem a strong, unmistakable primary color:

BLUE

* deep royal/space blue base
* bright cyan internal energy
* crisp blue/cyan highlights

PURPLE

* deep violet base
* bright magenta/violet core
* clearly different from blue

GREEN

* deep emerald base
* bright green internal energy
* stronger green edge lighting

YELLOW

* rich amber/gold base
* bright golden core
* avoid washing it out to white

PINK / MAGENTA

* deep hot-pink/magenta base
* bright pink core
* clearly separated from purple

IMPORTANT CONTRAST RULE

Do NOT make every gem simply:
“bright neon + white glow.”

Instead use:

DARKER SATURATED MATERIAL
+
BRIGHT COLORED CORE
+
CONTROLLED SPECULAR HIGHLIGHT

This will make the colors much more readable.

GEM EDGES

Strengthen each gem’s colored edge/rim.

The silhouette should remain clearly visible against the dark board.

Use:

* colored rim light
* subtle dark outer separation
* crisp crystal facets
* controlled internal glow

Avoid excessive bloom that makes the edges disappear.

INTERNAL CORE

Make the energy inside each crystal strongly color-specific.

The core should remain colored rather than becoming mostly white.

White highlights are okay, but they should be small and concentrated.

BOARD CONTRAST

Keep the board/background darker and more neutral so the gems naturally stand out.

Do NOT make the board brighter to compensate.

The gems should be the brightest gameplay objects.

DIFFERENTIATE SIMILAR COLORS

Pay special attention to:

BLUE vs PURPLE

PURPLE vs MAGENTA

YELLOW vs WHITE HIGHLIGHTS

GREEN vs CYAN

These must remain immediately distinguishable.

VFX COLOR MATCH

When a gem clears, its VFX must use the SAME color identity as the gem.

Blue gem → blue/cyan VFX
Purple gem → violet/magenta VFX
Green gem → emerald/green VFX
Yellow gem → gold/yellow VFX
Pink gem → pink/magenta VFX

Do not allow destruction VFX to wash the gem’s color into white.

ACCESSIBILITY / READABILITY

Test the board at the actual mobile size.

The player should be able to identify a gem’s color immediately without needing to inspect it closely.

Also make sure the gems remain distinguishable during:

* normal gameplay
* fast swaps
* falling
* cascades
* Mega Strike
* heavy VFX

PREMIUM LOOK

Do NOT make the gems look flat or cartoonish.

Keep:

* 3D crystal depth
* facets
* reflections
* internal energy
* premium materials
* cinematic highlights

The target is:

high-contrast crystalline gems with distinct luxury sci-fi color identities.

IMPORTANT

Inspect the existing gem CSS/rendering first.

Do NOT create another huge override stylesheet.

There may already be later CSS rules overriding earlier visual changes. Modify the actual active rules and consolidate conflicting overrides where appropriate.

After implementation, test every gem color together on the same board.

The final board should have strong visual color separation while retaining the premium glowing crystal appearance.
