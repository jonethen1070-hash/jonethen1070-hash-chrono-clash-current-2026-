Perform an EXTREME VISUAL + GRAPHIC POLISH PASS on Chrono Clash.

The goal is to make the game visually feel like a premium, modern, AAA-inspired sci-fi mobile game.

IMPORTANT:

* This is a visual/art-direction pass.
* DO NOT change gameplay mechanics.
* DO NOT change matching logic.
* DO NOT change scoring.
* DO NOT change turns.
* DO NOT change abilities.
* DO NOT change Mega Strike behavior.
* Do not remove working functionality.
* Preserve the current responsive mobile gameplay.
* Optimize everything for real-device performance.

1. OVERALL ART DIRECTION

Create a cohesive visual identity:

Premium futuristic cosmic battle arena
2.5D / dimensional presentation
dark metallic + glass materials
luminous crystalline gems
controlled cyan / blue / red / violet energy
cinematic lighting
deep contrast
high-end mobile-game finish

Avoid:

* flat UI
* cheap gradients
* generic neon everywhere
* excessive glow
* cartoonish visuals
* clutter
* unfinished placeholder-looking elements

Everything should feel like it belongs to the same game.

2. GAMEPLAY ENVIRONMENT

Upgrade the entire gameplay background.

Add visual depth:

* layered cosmic atmosphere
* subtle distant particles/stars
* dimensional environmental shapes
* dark metallic structures
* glass/energy surfaces
* subtle depth haze
* controlled light sources
* atmospheric glow around important gameplay areas

The environment should feel like a real futuristic battle arena rather than a flat background.

Keep the board as the visual focus.

3. BOARD

Make the board feel physically constructed.

Improve:

* dimensional frame
* metallic/glass materials
* depth
* bevels
* edge highlights
* internal reflections
* subtle emissive lighting
* shadows
* contact lighting
* layered frame geometry

The board should feel like a premium physical device floating inside the arena.

Avoid making the frame excessively bright.

4. GEM GRAPHICS

The gems are the HERO visual element.

Make them look like high-value futuristic energy crystals.

Improve:

* 3D depth
* faceted crystal geometry
* internal energy
* translucent edges
* realistic highlights
* reflections
* controlled bloom
* internal glow
* subtle animated energy
* depth shadows
* contact shadows

Every gem should look dimensional even on a small phone screen.

Colors must remain immediately distinguishable.

5. GEM MATERIAL QUALITY

Avoid the appearance of:

* flat colored icons
* simple circles
* generic glowing buttons

Aim for:
crystal + glass + energy core

The player should visually feel that every gem is a physical object containing energy.

6. LIGHTING

Introduce cinematic lighting hierarchy.

Primary:

* board
* gems
* player/opponent state
* important abilities

Secondary:

* environment
* background atmosphere

Use colored light selectively.

For example:

* blue/cyan energy around blue gameplay elements
* red energy around opposing/danger elements
* violet for cosmic/special abilities

Do not wash the entire screen in neon.

Upgrade the HUD to premium sci-fi quality.

Improve:

* health bars

* ability buttons

* typography
* spacing
* panels
* separators
* backgrounds

Use:

* glass panels
* subtle metallic borders
* restrained glow
* depth
* clean typography
* consistent icon treatment

The HUD should look designed rather than assembled from standard UI components.

8. BUTTONS

Make interactive controls visually premium.

States:

* normal
* pressed
* selected
* unavailable
* cooldown
* ready

Use subtle:

* depth
* highlight

Make abilities visually distinctive.

Each ability should have:

* unique icon treatment
* unique color identity
* premium button design

Mega Strike should feel especially powerful.

* main menu

Defeat:

16. MICRO GRAPHIC POLISH

Look for small visual imperfections:

* inconsistent spacing
* misaligned elements
* uneven padding
* awkward scaling
* jagged edges
* inconsistent glow
* poor contrast
* weak hierarchy
* elements touching screen edges
* UI overlapping gameplay
* icons that don’t visually match
* elements that look like placeholders

Fix all of these.

17. MOBILE RESPONSIVENESS

Test the visual layout at multiple phone sizes/orientations supported by the game.

Make sure:

* HUD doesn’t overlap
* board remains centered
* gems remain readable
* buttons remain accessible
* text doesn’t clip
* panels don’t overflow
* effects don’t obscure important information
* safe areas are respected

18. PERFORMANCE

Do not sacrifice performance for graphics.

Inspect:

* excessive shadows
* excessive blur
* excessive particles
* expensive filters
* unnecessary DOM layers
* repeated rendering
* memory-heavy effects

Use efficient techniques and reuse existing systems where possible.

Target smooth gameplay on real mobile hardware.

19. EXISTING CSS / STYLE ARCHITECTURE

Before changing styles, inspect the existing stylesheet architecture carefully.

There may be later override rules that supersede earlier visual changes.

Do not blindly append conflicting styles.

Find the actual active selectors and fix the correct rules.

Avoid creating another huge layer of unnecessary overrides.
