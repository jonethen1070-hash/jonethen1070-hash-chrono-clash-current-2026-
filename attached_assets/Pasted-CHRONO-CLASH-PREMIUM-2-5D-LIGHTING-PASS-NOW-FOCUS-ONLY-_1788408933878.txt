==================================================
LIGHTING GOAL
==================================================

Make Chrono Clash look like a PREMIUM 2.5D SCI-FI ARENA through lighting.

The lighting must create the illusion that the UI, board, gems and controls are physical objects existing in the same 3D space.

DEPTH MUST COME FROM LIGHT + SHADOW + MATERIAL.

Do NOT simply increase brightness or add neon glow.

==================================================
1. GLOBAL ARENA LIGHT
==================================================

Create a subtle dark cinematic base illumination.

The center gameplay area should remain readable.

Use soft atmospheric illumination behind the game elements.

The background should have:
- distant light
- subtle haze
- depth falloff
- faint particles
- controlled cosmic illumination

Keep the overall scene dark and premium.

==================================================
2. CYAN PLAYER LIGHT
==================================================

Create a soft cyan light source originating from the YOU side.

It should subtly illuminate:

- YOU panel
- attack bar
- Energy area
- left side of board housing
- nearby board edges
- nearby gems
- lower-left controls

The cyan light must have natural falloff.

Do NOT make the entire screen cyan.

==================================================
3. RED RIVAL LIGHT
==================================================

Create a soft crimson/red light source originating from the RIVAL side.

It should subtly illuminate:

- RIVAL panel
- rival attack bar
- right side of board housing
- nearby board edges
- nearby gems
- lower-right controls

Again, use natural falloff.

Do NOT flood the whole screen with red.

==================================================
4. BOARD LIGHTING
==================================================

The board is the main focal object.

Give the board a strong but controlled cyan rim light.

Create:

outer rim highlight
→ bevel highlight
→ darker recessed frame
→ subtle interior illumination
→ gem illumination

The board should look like a physical illuminated machine.

Add subtle contact shadows around the inner playfield.

==================================================
5. GEM LIGHTING
==================================================

Keep the existing crystalline gems.

Do NOT replace them.

Make each gem feel self-illuminated but physically grounded.

Use:

crystal emission
+
edge highlights
+
internal light
+
subtle contact shadow

The gem should illuminate its immediate socket slightly.

The glow must NOT spill excessively onto neighboring gems.

The crystals must remain sharply readable.

==================================================
6. GEM COLOR LIGHT
==================================================

Each gem type should have its own controlled light character.

CYAN:
cool crystalline emission

GREEN:
emerald energy emission

PURPLE:
violet crystal emission

PINK:
magenta energy emission

BLUE:
electric blue emission

GOLD:
warm energy emission

Keep the illumination subtle enough that the crystal geometry remains visible.

==================================================
7. HUD LIGHTING
==================================================

Apply environmental lighting to the HUD.

YOU:
cyan edge illumination.

RIVAL:
red edge illumination.

VS:
balanced cyan/red illumination.

Timer:
cool white/cyan illumination.

Attack bars:
subtle internal energy illumination.

Energy:
stronger internal cyan energy.

Energy Burst:
controlled green energy.

Mega Strike:
controlled crimson energy.

==================================================
8. DEPTH SHADOWS
==================================================

This is extremely important.

Add subtle shadows BETWEEN layers.

Examples:

YOU panel → shadow behind panel

Score module → shadow inside panel

Attack bar → recessed shadow

Energy reservoir → recessed shadow

Ability cards → shadow underneath chassis

Board → shadow behind housing

Gems → contact shadow inside sockets

Bottom controls → shadow behind their housings

The shadows must communicate DEPTH.

Do not use harsh black outlines.

==================================================
9. BEVEL LIGHTING
==================================================

Make bevels catch the light.

Raised surfaces should receive highlights.

Recessed surfaces should become darker.

Corners should catch subtle specular highlights.

This should make the interface appear manufactured from:

dark metal
+
smoked glass
+
crystal
+
energy materials.

==================================================
10. GLASS LIGHTING
==================================================

HUD glass should have subtle reflections.

Use:

dark translucent glass
+
soft reflection
+
edge highlight
+
inner shadow

Do NOT make the panels transparent enough to lose readability.

==================================================
11. CINEMATIC LIGHT BALANCE
==================================================

The brightest areas should naturally draw attention in this order:

1. gameplay gems
2. board frame
3. Energy / abilities
4. YOU / RIVAL
5. secondary controls
6. background

Do not make every element equally bright.

==================================================

- raised objects
- recessed objects
- illuminated objects
- objects sitting in front of other objects
- physical board depth
