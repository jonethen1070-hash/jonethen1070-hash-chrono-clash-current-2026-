==================================================
2. BOARD — MOST IMPORTANT
==================================================

The board is the hero object.

Make the board housing visibly THICK.

It must look like a real futuristic machine component.

Create:

- outer metallic shell
- thick cyan illuminated bevel
- darker side wall
- recessed inner playfield
- deep individual gem sockets
- subtle underside shadow

The cyan outer frame must not simply be a glowing border.

It needs to appear like LIGHT IS HITTING A PHYSICAL BEVEL.

Use different brightness on different edges.

Top/left edges:
stronger cool highlight.

Bottom/right edges:
darker falloff.

This should create the illusion of thickness.

==================================================
3. RECESSED PLAYFIELD
==================================================

The inside of the board must sit visibly BELOW the outer frame.

Do NOT let the playfield and outer frame appear on the same plane.

Add:

- inner bevel
- dark cavity
- contact shadow under the frame
- subtle internal reflection

The player should be able to visually distinguish:

FRAME
from
PLAYFIELD.

==================================================
4. GEM SOCKETS
==================================================

Every gem must sit inside a physical socket.

Do NOT make gems appear pasted onto the board.

Each socket should have:

- dark recessed cavity
- inner bevel
- subtle contact shadow
- tiny local reflection

The gem should appear to emerge FROM the socket.

==================================================
5. GEMS
==================================================

KEEP THE EXISTING GEM ARTWORK.

Do not replace the crystals.

Make them appear physically 3D through lighting.

Each gem needs:

TOP/FORWARD EDGE HIGHLIGHT
+
DARKER LOWER EDGE
+
CONTACT SHADOW
+
SUBTLE LOCAL REFLECTION
+
CONTROLLED INTERNAL EMISSION

The lighting must follow the gem's geometry.

Do not simply add a circular glow behind every gem.

Different gem colors should produce very subtle local reflections:

CYAN → cool blue reflection
GREEN → green reflection
RED/PINK → red/pink reflection
PURPLE → violet reflection
GOLD → warm gold reflection
BLUE → deep blue reflection

Keep the light localized.

13. LIGHT DIRECTION
==================================================

Use one consistent virtual lighting direction across the ENTIRE screen.

Primary:

Secondary:

Do NOT give every component its own unrelated glow.

==================================================
14. CONTACT SHADOWS
==================================================

This is critical.

Add subtle contact shadows wherever one object sits above another.

Especially:

- board frame → playfield
- gems → sockets
- emblems → player panels
- ability icons → ability panels
- bottom icons → control panels
- HUD modules → arena background

Contact shadows are what will make the screen stop looking like flat CSS.

==================================================

==================================================

Light must weaken with distance.

Avoid uniform screen-wide neon.
