Fix the performance slowdown caused specifically by MEGA BURST and ENERGY BLAST in Chrono Clash.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT weaken, remove, or slow down these abilities.
Do NOT reduce their gameplay impact.
Do NOT add delays to hide the performance issue.

The abilities should still feel BIG and powerful, but they must remain smooth when activated during fast gameplay or cascades.

FIRST:
Inspect the existing implementation of:

* Mega Burst
* Energy Blast
* their VFX
* particles
* screen effects
* glow/lighting
* trails
* explosions
* shake
* animation/tween loops
* timers
* DOM/canvas objects
* cleanup logic

Find the actual performance bottleneck before changing it.

1. STOP VFX STACKING

When Mega Burst or Energy Blast activates during an existing cascade, prevent expensive VFX from stacking uncontrollably.

If multiple effects are triggered at the same time:

* combine similar effects
* reuse existing effect objects
* cap duplicate particles
* prioritize the main ability effect

The main ability must remain visually dominant.

2. PARTICLE CAP

Add a hard upper limit for particles generated by Mega Burst and Energy Blast.

Do NOT spawn hundreds of individual particles when the ability hits many gems.

Instead:

* use fewer particles
* make each particle visually stronger
* use short-lived bursts
* reuse pooled particles

The effect should LOOK powerful without requiring a huge number of objects.

3. NO PARTICLE PER GEM

This is especially important.

If Mega Burst destroys 10, 20, or more gems:

DO NOT create a full expensive particle explosion for every gem.

Use:

* one primary explosion
* several lightweight secondary impacts
* a few prioritized gem impacts

The player should perceive a massive chain reaction, while the engine processes far fewer effects.

4. OPTIMIZE ENERGY BLAST

For Energy Blast:

* keep the main energy beam/blast
* keep the impact
* keep the satisfying screen response
* keep the premium sci-fi appearance

But simplify expensive secondary layers.

Avoid simultaneously stacking:

* multiple blur layers
* multiple large shadows
* many translucent overlays
* excessive bloom/glow
* unnecessary trails
* repeated screen flashes

Use short, controlled animation instead.

5. OPTIMIZE MEGA BURST

Mega Burst should feel explosive but extremely short.

Recommended structure:

0ms:
Immediate activation feedback.

0–100ms:
Main burst/impact.

100–220ms:
Primary particles + gem destruction.

220–350ms:
Secondary energy/cascade feedback.

After ~350ms:
Aggressively clean up temporary VFX.

Do not keep expensive effects alive after the ability has visually completed.

Adjust timing to match the existing game’s design if necessary.

6. SCREEN SHAKE

Inspect screen shake.

If Mega Burst/Energy Blast currently trigger multiple shake systems simultaneously, consolidate them into ONE shake event.

Do not allow:
Mega Burst shake
+
gem shake
+
cascade shake
+
screen impact shake

to run independently at the same time.

Combine them into one lightweight camera/screen response.

7. GLOW / BLUR

Inspect every glow/filter used by these abilities.

Large-area blur and filter effects can become expensive on mobile.

During the ability:

* keep the primary glow
* reduce unnecessary secondary glow layers
* avoid multiple large blurred elements
* use opacity/scale where possible instead of expensive filters

The ability should still look premium.

8. ANIMATION LOOP AUDIT

Check for requestAnimationFrame loops, timers, intervals, and tweens created by Mega Burst/Energy Blast.

Make sure every temporary animation:

* terminates correctly
* cannot duplicate indefinitely
* does not continue after the effect is invisible
* does not create another loop every time the ability is pressed

Rapidly activating abilities must NOT cause old animation loops to accumulate.

9. EFFECT POOLING

Pool frequently reused:

* particles
* sparks
* energy fragments
* impact effects
* blast rings
* trails

Reuse them rather than repeatedly creating and destroying large numbers of objects.

10. PRIORITY SYSTEM

During heavy gameplay, use this VFX priority:

HIGHEST:

1. Ability activation
2. Main Mega Burst / Energy Blast
3. Gem destruction feedback
4. Main cascade feedback

MEDIUM:
5. Secondary particles
6. Small impact sparks

LOW:
7. Decorative particles
8. Ambient effects

When performance is stressed, reduce LOW priority effects first.

Never sacrifice:

* input responsiveness
* gem movement
* ability activation feedback
* core match feedback

11. RAPID ACTIVATION TEST

Test these exact situations:

A. Mega Burst on an empty/normal board.

B. Mega Burst during a large cascade.

C. Energy Blast during a large cascade.

D. Mega Burst immediately followed by Energy Blast.

E. Energy Blast followed by another match.

F. Rapid repeated ability activation if the game allows it.

G. 30–60 seconds of fast gameplay with multiple abilities.

There must be NO progressive slowdown from accumulated VFX.

12. PERFORMANCE TARGET

The important target is:

60 FPS where the device can normally sustain 60 FPS.

More importantly:
NO noticeable input lag
NO stuttering when pressing Mega Burst
NO stuttering when pressing Energy Blast
NO animation queue buildup
NO VFX accumulation

If the device is under heavy load, gracefully reduce secondary VFX rather than slowing gameplay.

13. DO NOT CHANGE GAMEPLAY

Do NOT modify:

* damage
* scoring
* match rules
* ability rules
* cooldowns
* board logic
* gem behavior
* win/lose conditions

Only optimize how the existing abilities are rendered and animated.

FINAL FEEL:

MEGA BURST:
POWERFUL → INSTANT → EXPLOSIVE → SHORT → SMOOTH

ENERGY BLAST:
FAST → BRIGHT → IMPACTFUL → CLEAN → SMOOTH

The player should think:
“That was powerful.”

NOT:
“The game slowed down when I pressed it.”
