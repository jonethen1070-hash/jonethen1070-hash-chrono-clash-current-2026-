Chrono-Crystal Gen-3 prototype credits
======================================

PREVIEW ONLY. These files are not the live Chrono Clash soundtrack.

All four beds are arranged from one licensed instrumental:

  "Wraghstep [v2]" by Of Far Different Nature
  https://fardifferent.carrd.co/
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0

Gen-2 Defeat (this track's premium peak) is the sonic reference.
Lobby / Gameplay / Victory are recomposed from other sections of the
same production — not copies of the Defeat cue.

Chrono Clash motif overlay (A–E–C–D), arrangement, iPhone master,
and crystal gem swipe SFX are original to this prototype.
No vocals. Defeat is dark rematch energy, never sad.
